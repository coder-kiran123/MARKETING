3 How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery — To provide, manage, and improve our digital marketing services
  • Client Communication — To respond to inquiries, send project updates, and provide support
  • Billing & Payments — To process invoices, manage accounts, and handle payment disputes
  • Marketing — To send newsletters, case studies, and promotional content (only with your consent)
  • Analytics — To understand how our website is used and improve user experience
  • Legal Compliance — To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and contractual obligations
  • Security — To detect, prevent, and address fraud and security issues

We will never use your data in ways that are incompatible with the purposes listed above without your prior consent.

4 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Contract — Processing is necessary to perform the services you've engaged us for
  • Legitimate Interests — We process data to run and improve our business, provided your rights are not overridden
  • Consent — Where you have given explicit consent (e.g., email marketing sign-ups)
  • Legal Obligation — Where we are required to process data by law (e.g., tax records, fraud prevention)

Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

5 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data with trusted third parties only as necessary to deliver our services:

  • Advertising Platforms — Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn (for campaign execution)
  • Analytics Tools — Google Analytics, Hotjar (anonymized usage data)
  • Email Platforms — Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign (for email marketing campaigns)
  • Payment Processors — Stripe, Mercury Bank (for billing and invoicing)
  • Project Management — Notion, Slack, Asana (internal team collaboration)
  • Legal & Compliance — Accountants, legal advisors, regulatory bodies (only when required)

All third-party partners are bound by confidentiality agreements and are required to process data only on our documented instructions.

We may disclose your data if required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of KeyHub Solutions or others.

6 Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. We use the following types:

  • Essential Cookies — Required for the website to function (cannot be disabled)
  • Analytics Cookies — Help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Marketing Cookies — Used to deliver relevant ads and track campaign performance (e.g., Meta Pixel)
  • Preference Cookies — Remember your settings and preferences across visits

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies at any time through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features.

7 International Data Transfers

KeyHub Solutions serves clients in 30+ countries. As a result, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States.

When transferring data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by relevant data protection authorities
  • Adequacy decisions by the European Commission where applicable
  • Binding Corporate Rules or other approved transfer mechanisms

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the right to request information about the specific safeguards applied to your data transfers.

8 Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected:

  • Client Data — Retained for the duration of the engagement plus 5 years for legal and tax purposes
  • Prospect/Lead Data — Retained for 2 years from last interaction; deleted if no engagement occurs
  • Website Analytics — Aggregated data retained for up to 26 months
  • Marketing Emails — Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Financial Records — Retained for 7 years as required by tax regulations

When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized in accordance with our data destruction policy.

10 Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business professionals and are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately at privacy@keyhubsolutions.com and we will delete it promptly.

11 Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted via our website
  • Role-based access controls — only authorized personnel can access personal data
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) on all internal systems and client platforms
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure cloud storage with encrypted backups
  • Employee privacy and security training

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we use best practices, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by law.
